Organisation

How we deliver
Delivery

Five ways to learn.
One standard of delivery.

Live online

Instructor-Led Virtual

Scheduled live sessions with a certified trainer, exercises and Q&A. Same syllabus and exam preparation as the classroom, without the travel.

In person

Classroom Training

Face-to-face delivery in Hyderabad or at your premises, with printed materials, group exercises and direct trainer access throughout.

Self-paced

E-Learning

Structured modules you work through on your own schedule, with practice questions and mock exams for certification readiness.

For teams

Corporate Training

Private cohorts for your organisation — flexible scheduling, a dedicated coordinator, consolidated invoicing and group pricing.

Bespoke

Customised Programs

Built from a skills-gap assessment: tailored curriculum, your own case studies, blended formats and progress reporting for L&D.

Exam focus

Certification Preparation

Exam-technique sessions, mock papers and voucher guidance aligned to the current syllabus of each certification body.
